# Photo Town: what's in the world, and 100 proposed additions

As of version 168: **458 buildable models** (430 unique after duplicates), 102 model files, 524 texture images,
300 camera views. Part 1 lists what exists, grouped by place. Part 2 proposes 100 additions that fill gaps
around those places.

One standing rule to flag: so far everything has been built from your photos. Part 2 is generic everyday stuff
that would make the town feel lived in. Each item says which of your photo textures it can reuse. For anything
you'd rather base on a real reference, a photo of it would make it better. Strike out what you don't want.
